You've heard the outcome promise before. Then the invoices arrived on schedule — and the outcomes didn't.

Outcome-based pricing means the fee describes how we get paid — not how we market.

Microsoft provides the technology platform. congruentX accepts accountability for turning that platform into agreed technical and business outcomes. We define those outcomes with you up front, attach congruentX's fees to achieving them, and verify the results through telemetry you can see. For agents, the work does not stop at deployment — we monitor, govern, and tune them so value does not stop at go-live.

What this is not: it is not unlimited upside, and it is not a discount scheme. A $1M engagement remains a $1M engagement. What changes is when payment releases — attached to specific, verifiable outcome milestones instead of hours billed or calendar dates reached. Many firms market "outcomes" while billing for time. The test is simple: ask any partner to show you the milestone that releases their next invoice.

How it works

What we bet on · what we track · what we do

What you stop paying for on faith

Fees attach here — what we bet on
  • Technical outcomes live. Platform in production, data migrated and validated, integrations cut over, legacy retired.
  • Agents in production. Named agents deployed on live data, available to target users, emitting telemetry from day one.
  • Your people adopting. Named users active in the flow of work, measured as a rate — with thresholds agreed before we start measuring.
Why these: they are the drivers the business results depend on — and they are verifiable. If the system isn't live, used, and trusted, no downstream number can be.

What you see, every quarter

Reported openly — what we track
  • Operating movement. Follow-up speed, data completeness, handoff quality, response time, risk detected earlier.
  • Business results. Win rate, cycle time, retention, CSAT, cost-to-serve — against baselines set together at the start.
  • The honest context. Market shifts, staffing changes, pricing moves — the factors that also move these numbers, named alongside them.
One scoreboard, both logos. Telemetry originates in your environment, not ours. There is no partner-only version of performance.

What never becomes your problem

Continuing — what we do, not ship and leave
  • Monitor and govern every deployed agent: usage, quality, acceptance, override rates.
  • Tune and improve when signals drift — remediation is our work, at our cost, before it becomes your surprise.
  • Review quarterly. Outcomes, metrics, and next interventions in a standing business review — not a support ticket queue.
The agent rule: an agent is not done when it ships. It is done when it keeps producing the result it was built for.

The commercial model in one view

Four kinds of promise, visible on the page

Verified milestones

Fee releases on proof

Platform live, data validated, agents in production. Verified through evidence you can audit — deployment records, reconciliation reports, telemetry. If it isn't verified, it isn't invoiced.

Adoption milestones

Fee releases on use

Your named users, active at an agreed rate, for an agreed window. Measured fairly: rates within a named team, so your staffing decisions never distort the measure — in either direction.

Operating milestones

Fee releases on the work changing

The behaviors that predict the business results: follow-up within SLA, data completeness, handoffs completed, agent output quality in band — each a rate within the named team, thresholds agreed before measurement begins.

Business results

Tracked and reported

Win rate, CSAT, retention, cost-to-serve. These are why the work exists. We baseline them, instrument them, and report them quarterly with the surrounding context — honestly, on a scoreboard both parties see.

Not verified, not invoiced.
Each layer predicts the next: live capability makes adoption possible, adoption makes the work change, changed work moves the business numbers. If a milestone is missed, the tranche is held, congruentX remediates, and the milestone is remeasured. Payment releases on verified achievement — never on time passed.
